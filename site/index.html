Not another tower-stacker.

Blockora replaces "stack blocks, watch numbers go up" with real construction logic: structural physics, production chains, and a city that reacts to what you actually build.

🧱

Modular Construction

Every building starts with a foundation and grows one module at a time — farms, power plants, and data centers all built from the same connected grid.

🏗️

Real Structural Physics

Load and support actually matter. Overload a module and it — and whatever it's holding up — comes down. No invisible walls, just consequences.

⚙️

Production Chains

Energy feeds servers, water feeds greenhouses, synergy combos multiply output. What you place next to what changes everything.

🌍

Live World Events

Demand shifts in real time — a data boom or an energy shortage changes what's worth building today versus tomorrow.

The World Project
